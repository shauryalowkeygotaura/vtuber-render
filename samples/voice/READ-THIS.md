# Voice clone take: how to record (about 5 minutes)

**Where:** the quietest room. Fan and AC off, door shut. Soft stuff around (bed, curtains) beats a bare room.
**Mic:** your phone's voice recorder, held 15-20 cm from your mouth, slightly below the chin (no breath pops). No earphone mic.
**How:** talk like you do in reels: same energy, same speed. Don't "read". Small pauses between lines are fine.
**Length:** 60-90 s of you talking. One clean take beats three noisy ones.
**Save as:** `Code/vtuber-render/samples/voice/take1.m4a` (or .wav). If you also do a Hinglish take, save it as `take2-hinglish.m4a`.

## Read these (mix of tones on purpose)

1. Okay so, I built a thing this week and honestly it kind of broke my brain.
2. My dashboard said everything was down. It wasn't. My internet was just lying to me.
3. Here's the part nobody tells you: the hard part isn't the code, it's knowing what to build.
4. Wait. WAIT. Did that actually just work on the first try?
5. Three steps. One, find the problem. Two, make it stupidly simple. Three, ship it before you overthink it.
6. Is it perfect? No. Is it live? Yes. That's the whole game.
7. I'm sixteen, I'm in Delhi, and I build AI that answers phones for businesses.
8. If you're still scrolling, this one's for you: start before you feel ready.
9. Hmm... let me think about that for a second.
10. Follow if you want to see if this actually makes money. I'm documenting all of it.

Hinglish take (optional): say lines 1, 3, 6 and 8 the way you'd actually say them to a friend.
